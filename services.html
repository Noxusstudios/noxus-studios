Inside
the retainer

Six things ship every month.

/ 01

Design iteration

Surgical layout, nav, and styling adjustments. New conversion frames without tearing out the foundation. The page evolves; the brand stays consistent.

/ 02

Bug fixes, same day

Broken cart? Glitchy nav? Form silently failing? Diagnosed same-day, fixed before the day's out — not parked in a ticket queue.

/ 03

New features, shipped

Conversion sections, quizzes, calculators, filters, gated content, sticky CTAs. If you can describe the behaviour, it ships inside the month.

/ 04

Stack integrations

Hooking your site into the rest of the stack: HubSpot, Notion, Klaviyo, Stripe, Plausible — whatever your ops actually run on. Wired up, tested end-to-end, documented.

/ 05

Conversion audits

Monthly look at what visitors are actually doing on the site. Friction points flagged, prioritized, fixed at the source. A/B tests on the highest-leverage pages.

/ 06

AI & automation

Chatbots that actually know your services and pricing. Automations that handle the busy work — lead routing, content sync, scheduled reports. Less hype, more time back.
